Feature: Support to unreachable coast allowed

Feature: Support can be cut with other coast
    Given
        England:
            fleet in Irish Sea supports movement of North Atlantic Ocean to Mid-Atlantic Ocean
            fleet in North Atlantic Ocean moves to Mid-Atlantic Ocean

        France:
            fleet in Spain (nc) supports hold of Mid-Atlantic Ocean
            fleet in Mid-Atlantic Ocean holds

        Italy:
            fleet in Gulf of Lyon moves to Spain (sc)
    Then
        Spain fails
        Mid-Atlantic Ocean fails
        Gulf of Lyon fails
        Mid-Atlantic Ocean is dislodged
